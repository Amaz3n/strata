## Why and actual evidence

Arc's `lib/services/receivable-adjustments.ts` creates only invoice-linked credit_memo/write_off adjustments through a service-only command and flags the invoice mapping needs_review with instructions to apply matching credit externally. `lib/services/customer-refunds.ts` explicitly records an offline refund: it authorizes payment.release on the actual payment/project, rejects non-manual payments, calls `record_manual_customer_refund_atomic`, and audits only nonduplicate results. Provider refund imports cannot use that wrapper by changing a QBO/Stripe payment to manual.

Actual effective `create_receivable_adjustment_atomic` in `20260908030000_accounting_d2_routine_consumers.sql:102` requires an issued client-visible invoice, rejects earnest deposits, caps adjustment against current open invoice balance and bounds tax reversals. Its early existing idempotency return does not compare the new request's details; any new domain/retry wrapper must bind a canonical command hash before reusing an identity. Existing `receivable_adjustments.invoice_id` is NOT NULL. There is no canonical standalone/unapplied customer credit/application domain yet.

Actual `invoice_paid_cents` includes cash receipts minus payment_reversals plus posted receivable_adjustments. `record_manual_customer_refund_atomic` locks its payment, checks unused deposit amount and pending/succeeded reversals, and records the returned cash through `record_payment_reversal_atomic`. QBO/Stripe provider payments remain rejected. Parent selected existing domain baseline passed 10/10 in `/private/tmp/q14-parent-existing-domain-baseline.log`; that fixture uses minimal synthetic tables, not complete provider or current Books acceptance.

An additional executable negative uses exact effective `sync_qbo_invoice_opening_payment` and invoice_paid_cents definitions, exact D2 trigger statement and a service-role update on minimal required synthetic tables. A $100 imported invoice, no cash, and a $20 posted credit_memo (or write_off) correctly has $80 balance before the trigger. Updating that balance creates a $20 qbo_opening_balance payment; invoice_paid_cents then counts $40 cleared AR. Both cases reproduce in `/private/tmp/q14-parent-opening-credit-characterization.test.js`, 2/2 passes, log of the same basename and `/private/tmp/q14-parent-opening-credit-evidence.json`. This proves the actual opening routine conflates noncash reductions with cash on that seam. It does not prove a complete live import/Books ledger outcome; no financial posting/recalculation collaborators, provider or MVCC acceptance were executed. The implemented workflow must close that seam through actual import/recalculation/current-trigger fixtures rather than just subtracting a guessed remote credit total.

`provider.ts` and `provider-contract.ts` have vendor credit capability/methods but no customer credit/refund methods. `accounting-enqueue.ts` lists invoice, payment, project_expense, vendor_bill and bill_payment only. CDC_ENTITIES currently excludes CreditMemo/RefundReceipt. The signed webhook normalizer recognizes those names, but the actual worker's final branch marks unhandled entities ignored. Parsing a webhook is not customer-credit support.

Books `projector.ts:1012` projects each posted receivable_adjustment with invoice-derived income allocations, tax, project and date. `posting-rules.ts:461` posts credit/write-off separately from cash; `:894` posts a payment reversal separately. `fact-drafts.ts`, source revisions, projection receipts, native ownership and opening inventory also consume these shapes. Reusing an ordinary invoice payment or generic journal silently bypasses those ownership/balance contracts. Read current effective period, authority and source triggers before proposing extensions.
